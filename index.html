    Three steps to a plan

    1. Find a place

      Filter by beaches, mountains, culture or food, or switch on "best this month" to see only places in season.

    2. Join the dots

      Add stops to your trip and watch the route draw itself across the globe. Reorder them until it flows.

    3. Know the numbers

      Days, distance between stops and a rough budget add up as you go. Share the trip with a link.
